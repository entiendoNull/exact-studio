# LLP 0005: UI principles

**Type:** Principles
**Status:** Active
**Systems:** UI
**Author:** Jacob Clausen / Claude
**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Related:** LLP 0000, LLP 0002

Said by Jacob while building v1 (2026-11-11).

## Space. Embrace it.

Tight UI is hard to use and looks cluttered. When in doubt, add room. Leave space for the
scrollbar so content is never clipped.

## Calm by default

- The right sidebar was too noisy. Settings live in sections you can collapse.
- The left sidebar folds the same way: Simulators, Displays, Windows and Library each collapse, and the app remembers it.
- Crop is a mode, not a menu (see below).
- Both sidebars show and hide with a smooth animation.
- The selection's settings can be docked, floating in their own movable window, a lightbox over
  the video, or on the left (which closes the left sidebar).

## Crop on the picture

Crop works on the picture itself, for every recording. **Crop** under the preview shows the whole
recorded frame at the playhead, uncropped, with the crop lit over it:

- a thirds grid inside the crop, and the rest dimmed;
- a handle at each corner and edge: drag a corner to move two sides, an edge to move one;
- drag inside to move the crop as a whole;
- letting go saves it. Reset gives back the whole frame; Done returns to the edit.

It replaced the old Trim Top / Bottom / Left / Right menu, which only showed for devices.

## Add where it goes

Said by Jacob on 2026-11-13. One Add menu in the toolbar held everything, and it was not clear
what an item would change.

- Each row in the timeline has its own **+**, and it adds that row's kind of thing at the
  playhead: an effect on the video, a move, a text or an image, an effect on one image, an
  effect on the whole frame.
- So the toolbar stays short: play, Split Clip, Templates, Crop.
- An empty row says what its + does.
- Every + sits in one column at the end of the labels, just left of the track. Nothing in the
  label column reaches into the track, so the group headers say only "ON TOP" and "WHOLE FRAME".
- A + does not refuse because of the playhead. Off an image, its + adds near the image's start
  and moves the playhead there.

## Fold everything, pick a size

- Every group folds: the video's rows under the Clip, "On top of the video", "The whole frame",
  and each image's own rows. A folded group still shows where its things are: slim
  bars in the track, on up to three lines so things at one time stay apart.
- Adding or selecting something opens the group it is in.
- The rows have three heights, picked in the corner of the timeline and remembered. People
  differ: some want them tall, some compact.

## Direct and easy

- Click a block to select it. Drag it from where you grabbed it; it must not jump.
- Space plays and pauses. The play button shows the real state.
- Effects are picked from a menu. Templates are one click away.
- Drag the video in the preview to place it. Drag the handle to resize the preview.

## Looks

- Dark, plain, and polished. Many gradients, plus your own.
- Text in a pill is centered both ways.
- Our own icons and markers, never copied from other apps.
