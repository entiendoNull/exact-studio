# LLP 0005: UI principles

**Type:** Principles
**Status:** Active
**Systems:** UI
**Author:** Jacob Clausen / Claude
**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Related:** LLP 0000, LLP 0002

Said by Jacob while building v1 (2026-11-11).

## Space. Embrace it.

Tight UI is hard to use and looks cluttered. When in doubt, add room. Leave space for the
scrollbar so content is never clipped.

## Calm by default

- The right sidebar was too noisy. Settings live in sections you can collapse.
- The left sidebar folds the same way: Simulators and Library each collapse, and the app remembers it.
- What to record and how sits in one card: four source buttons and the System Sound switch.
- Crop is a mode, not a menu (see below).
- Both sidebars show and hide with a smooth animation.
- The selection's settings can be docked, floating in their own movable window, a lightbox over
  the video, or on the left (which closes the left sidebar). The ⋯ at the inspector's top holds
  **Show Settings**, which picks one; it is not called "placement", which is the video's.
- The inspector explains nothing in paragraphs: the controls say what they do. With nothing
  selected it shows the Frame, and one line says to select something in the timeline.
- Nothing destructive sits beside a routine control. Clear Edits is in Edit and the inspector's ⋯.
  Delete Recording is in File, the inspector's ⋯ and the Library row's right-click menu.
- The floating panel stays over the preview: it never covers the bars or the timeline. Where it
  was left is remembered, but a smaller window or a sidebar opened again moves it back in sight.
- A picker shows the value that plays, never a blank: a text with no animation set shows Pop in,
  Fade out and Still.

## Crop on the picture

Crop works on the picture itself, for every recording. **Crop** on the viewer bar shows the whole
recorded frame at the playhead, uncropped, with the crop lit over it:

- a thirds grid inside the crop, and the rest dimmed;
- a handle at each corner and edge: drag a corner to move two sides, an edge to move one;
- drag inside to move the crop as a whole;
- letting go saves it. Reset gives back the whole frame; Done returns to the edit.

It replaced the old Trim Top / Bottom / Left / Right menu, which only showed for devices.

## Three bars

Chosen by Jacob on 2026-11-18 (Option A of the layout options). One edit bar held play, Split
Clip, Templates and Crop with equal weight, so all of them read as "the whole piece".

Each area has its own bar, and a control sits on the bar of what it changes, as in Final Cut Pro:

- **The window toolbar: the whole piece.** Templates and Export, and the two sidebar switches.
- **The viewer bar, under the preview: the video.** A gold VIDEO chip, in the Clip's colour, says
  the scope. Crop and Place (where the video sits; with moves, a move at the playhead) on the left;
  play and the time (video time / length) in the middle; Motion Paths, a view switch, at the right.
- **The timeline bar, on top of the timeline: the timeline.** Split Clip and a visible Split All
  Lanes, Undo and Redo; on the right the zoom buttons and the length.
- The timeline's rows are one tree: a VIDEO header holds the Clip, its Sound, Position and Effects,
  as ON TOP holds the texts and images. VIDEO folds the rows under the Clip; the Clip stays.

## Add where it goes

Said by Jacob on 2026-11-13. One Add menu in the toolbar held everything, and it was not clear
what an item would change.

- Each row in the timeline has its own **+**, and it adds that row's kind of thing at the
  playhead: an effect on the video, a move, a text or an image, an effect on one image, an
  effect on the whole frame, a sound.
- So the window toolbar stays short (see "Three bars").
- A + adds items, never lanes. Lanes are made in the lane's ⋯ menu.
- Each + menu starts with a line that says where the item lands and what it changes:
  "Add to Effects 1 at 0:21.5 · Changes the video only". Long lists go in submenus:
  Effects has Zoom ›, Speed ›, Look ›, Mask and 3D Turn; Position has Add Move Here,
  Motion Patterns › and 3D Patterns ›.
- A row with one choice adds at once: the + on a Text lane adds a text, on an Image lane an image,
  on a Sound lane or the SOUND header a sound.
- A lane's + adds on that lane. When the item has no room there, it goes on the first lane of its
  kind with room, or on a new lane, as a drag below the last lane does.
- An empty row says what its + does.
- Every + sits in one column at the end of the labels, just left of the track. Nothing in the
  label column reaches into the track, so the group headers say only "ON TOP", "SOUND" and "WHOLE FRAME".
- A + does not refuse because of the playhead. Off an image, its + adds near the image's start
  and moves the playhead there.

## The lane menu

Chosen by Jacob on 2026-11-18 (Option A of the layout options). A lane was removed by a small ×
at the far end of its track, far from its name, and a lane could not be added where it was wanted.

- **+ is the item, ⋯ is the lane.** Each lane's header has both, in one column at the end of the
  label: + then ⋯. A right-click on the header opens the same menu as the ⋯.
- The menu: New *Kind* Lane Above and Below (the lane's own kind), Move Lane Up and Down, and
  Delete Lane. Lane order is draw order: Image 1 draws in front of Image 2.
- Delete Lane says what goes with it ("Delete Lane and Its 2 Texts…") and asks first when the
  lane holds anything. The last Effects lane cannot go: the item is off, with the reason under it.
- Each is one undo step: "New Text Lane", "Move Lane Up", "Delete Lane".
- The group headers' ⋯ (ON TOP, SOUND, WHOLE FRAME) make a new lane of their kinds at the end.
- Lanes have no names of their own yet; Rename needs a field in `edits.json`.

## Fold everything, pick a size

- Every group folds: VIDEO (the rows under the Clip), ON TOP, SOUND, WHOLE FRAME,
  and each image lane's own rows. A folded group still shows where its things are: slim
  bars in the track, on up to three lines so things at one time stay apart.
- Adding or selecting something opens the group it is in.
- The rows have three heights, picked in the corner of the timeline and remembered. People
  differ: some want them tall, some compact.

## Zoom the timeline

Asked for by Jacob on 2026-11-13: zoom with the mouse wheel and with buttons, and every row follows.

- A mouse wheel zooms, around the time under the pointer. A trackpad never zooms: it scrolls,
  sideways in time and up and down through the rows. A trackpad's scroll is too easy to start by
  accident, and a mouse has no other way to zoom. A pinch zooms on both.
- Shift with the mouse wheel slides the timeline sideways.
- The buttons sit left of the length: −, +, Fit, and a mouse switch. The switch turns wheel zoom off,
  and then the wheel scrolls the rows as before. The app remembers it.
- Zoom is how the timeline looks, not part of the edit. It is not in `edits.json`, the export,
  the CLI or the skill, and every recording opens showing the whole of it.
- Nothing zoomed is drawn over the labels, the +s or the lane sizes. They stay where they are.
- While playing, the view pages on when the playhead reaches its edge. A click never moves it.

## Show what moves

Asked for by Jacob on 2026-11-18: with several moves it was hard to picture where the video goes.

- The preview draws the path of the moves: the video's, or the selected image's when an image is selected.
- Where it is now: a solid teal outline and a dot. Where it starts: a small hollow dot.
- Each move's landing: a dashed frame at the size it lands, a diamond at its middle, and a label
  "1 · Smooth · 0:08.9" (its number in time order, its style, its time).
- Teal arrows go from landing to landing in order, bent a little so a move back does not cover the move out.
- The selected move is bright (white frame, white diamond, white label); the others fade.
- The frames come from the renderer's own placement: base, padding, frame shape, a zoom at that
  time, and the 3D turn as SceneKit draws it. A test checks each against a rendered frame.
- Drag a dashed frame, its diamond or its label to change where that move lands. One undo step, "Move Landing".
- Hidden while playing and while cropping. Never in the export, `frame` or the CLI: only the preview draws it.
- **Motion Paths** at the right end of the viewer bar and View ▸ Show Motion Paths turn it on and off. On by default and
  remembered, like the timeline's zoom. It is a view, not part of `edits.json`.
- No zoom frame: a zoom follows the cursor, and the preview at the playhead already shows it zoomed.

## Direct and easy

- Click a block to select it. Drag it from where you grabbed it; it must not jump.
- Space plays and pauses. The play button shows the real state.
- Effects are picked from a menu. Templates are one click away.
- Drag the video in the preview to place it. Drag the handle to resize the preview.
- Clear Edits and Delete ask first, as a Mac app does: an alert that names the recording and what
  goes, its button red, Cancel the default. Everything else acts at once, and ⌘Z takes it back.

## Looks

- Dark, plain, and polished. Many gradients, plus your own.
- Text in a pill is centered both ways.
- Our own icons and markers, never copied from other apps.
